Passenger Etiquette & Rules

No ID, No Ride

Always carry your valid university Student ID card. It is mandatory for boarding.

Be Punctual

Buses depart exactly on time. Please arrive at the pickup point 5 minutes early.

Queue Policy

Maintain a disciplined queue. Seats are filled on a first-come-first-serve basis.

Reserved Seating

Please respect the designated seating arrangements for faculty and students.

Emergency & Lost Items

For immediate assistance or to report lost items, please contact the Transport Supervisor
